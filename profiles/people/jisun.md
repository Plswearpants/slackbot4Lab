## Abstract

Jisun is a senior, hands-on member of the lab who for years has served as the group's principal instrument operator and troubleshooter, currently focused on building out the 4-probe system's electronics (switch box, Nanonis integration, wiring) while continuing to run and maintain the Tesla (Createc/Omicron) and Beast STM/AFM systems and support the Omicron/OMBE setup. Her sample work has spanned Au(111)/coin gold and PtAs/Ca-cluster surfaces early on, through FeSe, ZrTe5, CaPt, AuSn4, and NbSe2, to her more recent long-running LaSbTe (and K-doped LaSbxTe2-x) studies pursued jointly with ARPES collaborators. She is expert in noise and grounding diagnostics, tip conditioning and field-emission treatment, cryogenic (LN2/LHe) logistics and cooldown/bake-out procedures, and low-level electronics/Nanonis troubleshooting, and colleagues routinely defer to her judgment on instrument health, tip quality, and measurement scheduling. Beyond measurements, she regularly contributes to lab infrastructure—vacuum hardware design, sample-holder engineering, and coordination with vendors and facilities—making her a central, trusted problem-solver across nearly every system in the group.
